How it works

1

Input

Your text is sent to the FastAPI inference endpoint.

2

Preprocessing

Lowercased, cleaned, tokenized into integers, and padded to 50 tokens.

3

BiGRU inference

A two-layer bidirectional GRU reads the sequence forwards and backwards.

4

Prediction

Softmax produces a probability for each of the six emotion classes.
